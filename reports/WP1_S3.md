## Goal of the step
L2a: a Kalman filter fusing IMU, odometry and GPS, with a per-fix NIS chi-square test as the layer score; check its consistency, measure how much of each attack the filter absorbs, test a gated variant, evaluate on KITTI / AV-GPS / UAV and report what it adds on top of B2 + L1.

A3 clean-run FA on KITTI test at the val 1% budget <= 1.5% (both variants). A4 per attack "beats B2" iff run-level detection >= 5 points higher with clean-run FA <= B2 + 0.5 point; target: drift 0.2-0.5 m/s detected within 30 s in >= 60% of runs at <= 1% FA. A8 UAV spoofing: alarm within 5 s of 118.0 s, FA before 117 s <= 2%, parameters from the benign flight. A9 "L2a adds on top of B2 + L1" iff AUC +0.01 or an attack +5 points row-level, with combined FA <= 1.5%.

### Models
State x = [e, n, ve, vn, we, wn]: position, velocity, and the receiver error w (first-order Gauss-Markov; the stream's receiver error is correlated over ~10 s, so a white-noise GPS model would make the NIS wrong).

Process, dt = fix interval, a = IMU acceleration (nav frame, trapezoid mean of two fixes; UAV: mean over the last second):
- p_k = p_{k-1} + v_{k-1} dt + a dt^2 / 2;  v_k = v_{k-1} + a dt;  w_k = phi^dt w_{k-1} + eta
- Q = q^2 [[dt^4/4, dt^3/2],[dt^3/2, dt^2]] per axis for (p, v); Q_w = (1 - phi^(2dt)) sigma_w^2.

Measurements (independent, R diagonal; EKF linearisation):
- GPS position z = p + w + eps, R = r_pos^2 (2 dims)
- GPS Doppler z = v + eps, R = sd^2 (2 dims, when available)
- odometry speed z = |v| + eps, R = so^2 + (0.02 z)^2 (wheel-scale error treated as noise; calibrating it is S4), H = [ve, vn] / |v|
- compass heading z = atan2(ve, vn) + eps, R = (2 deg)^2, wrapped innovation, only when speed > 1 m/s (H = [vn, -ve] / |v|^2)

Update in Joseph form. Per fix: nu = z - h(x^-), S = H P^- H' + R, NIS = nu' S^-1 nu ~ chi2(m), m = active measurement dims (4-6). Layer score = -log P(chi2_m >= NIS), then the standard validation ECDF calibration and tie-safe threshold.

Gated variant: if NIS > chi2_m(level) the GPS block (position + Doppler) is left out of that update and of all following ones (dead-reckoning from the last trusted state; odometry and heading keep correcting velocity) until 3 consecutive fixes have NIS < chi2_m(95%).

### Where the noise parameters come from (and what was tuned)
Specs (fixed): Doppler 0.05 m/s, odometry 0.05 m/s + 2% scale, heading 2 deg, white position noise 0.3 m (stream documentation of S1 / v2). Tuned on KITTI TRAIN clean runs only: Gauss-Markov (phi 0.897, sigma 0.981 m, from reported minus true position; a real system would take them from static receiver data) and the single scalar q_acc = 0.1 m/s^2 (grid, mean NIS / dim closest to 1). Gate level 0.99: grid on VAL attack runs; the primary variant was chosen on VAL (ungated 0.833 vs gated 0.828 mean run-level detection, so **ungated is primary**; both are reported). AV-GPS (no Doppler, no IMU acceleration; position, speed, heading only): q_acc 1.0, r_pos 6 m, so 0.2 by grid on TRAIN sessions; the criterion was changed from mean NIS to "fraction above chi2 95% closest to 5%" after seeing that glitches dominate the mean (train data only, before any test run). UAV (no odometry, no heading): q_acc 0.1, sigma_w 1 m, r_pos 0.5, Doppler 0.15 m/s by grid on the benign (calibration) flight, threshold 1% budget on the benign flight.

## Beat-or-not vs B2, stated plainly
- At the val-calibrated 1% operating point the EKF has 2.6% false alarms on clean test runs against 0.4% for B2, so its higher run-level detection is not a like-for-like win and rule A4 is not satisfied.
- At EQUAL false alarms (diagnostic only, oracle threshold on the test clean runs, 1%; not deployable): the EKF beats B2 clearly on **Doppler-consistent spoof (87% vs 40% of runs)** and **meaconing (71% vs 46%)**; it is **the same as B2 on drift (39 vs 36%), accelerating drift (44 vs 43%) and jump (100% both)**; it is **worse on freeze (79 vs 89%)**. Slow drift < 0.5 m/s within 30 s: 14% vs 14% (target 60% NOT reached).
- Gating does not help detection at equal FA (drift 33%, Doppler 77%, meaconing 64%, slow drift 0%): after a hold, dead-reckoning error makes the NIS high on clean data too (clean-run FA 6.3% at the val budget).

## Absorption (the physics point)
The ungated filter follows a self-consistent spoof: with the Doppler-consistent spoof the estimate error at +30 s is 99% of the reported error, with meaconing 97%. It alarms at the transition (median delay 1 s) and then the innovation returns to normal, which is why row-level detection of these attacks is only 66% / 61% while run-level is 97% / 79%. Position-only attacks are partly absorbed (drift 43% of the reported error after 30 s, accelerating drift 38%, freeze 49%), because Doppler and IMU keep disagreeing with the drifting position. Gated: 5% (drift), 5% (accelerating drift), 3% (freeze), 16% (Doppler spoof), 18% (meaconing) after 30 s; the held dead-reckoning stays within 2-6 m of the truth at +30 s (odometry + heading), and row-level detection of Doppler spoof rises to 82%, meaconing 80%, jump 100%. Cost: after any false alarm the hold needs 3 consistent fixes and dead-reckoning drifts, so clean FA rises (6.3% vs 2.6%). The odometry-scale state and a hold-release/re-initialisation rule (S4) are the way to reduce this.
