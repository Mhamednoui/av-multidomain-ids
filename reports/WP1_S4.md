# WP1 S4: L2b CUSUM / SPRT on innovations, L2c online odometry-scale calibration, LODO calibration

Date: 2026-10-20    Worker: sonnet (WP1)

## Goal of the step
L2b: sequential tests (CUSUM, which contains the SPRT) on normalised EKF innovations and on a dead-reckoning branch that does not absorb GPS, aimed at slow drift (< 0.5 m/s detected within 30 s in >= 60% of runs) and at absorption. L2c: odometry scale learned online while GPS is trusted, frozen when a layer is suspicious. New binding policy from the supervisor: thresholds by leave-one-drive-out (LODO) over the 16 train+val drives, reported side by side with val-only calibration.

## Acceptance rule (notes/WP1_gps.md, "2026-10-19 S4", written before any S4 result; two disclosed edits: freeze level 4 -> 8 made before any real-data result; kappa grid extended after the first dev pass; see notes)
B1 tests pass. B2 scale converges on clean data (median error at 30 s <= 0.7%; 60 s <= 0.5% if runs are long enough). B3 freezing prevents dragging (error at onset+30 s <= 0.5 x without freezing and <= 1.0%). B4 new layers' clean-run FA on test <= 1.5% with LODO calibration. B5 slow drift (0.2-0.5 m/s) detected within 30 s in >= 60% of runs at FA <= 1.5%. B6 beats B2 (>= +5 points run level and FA <= B2 + 0.5 point, both LODO). B7 adds on top of B2 + L1 + L2a (AUC +0.01 or an attack +5 points row level, combined FA <= 1.5%). B8 frozen DR branch absorbs <= 30% of Doppler spoof and meaconing. B9 UAV alarm within 5 s of 118.0 s, FA before 117 s <= 2%.

### Layers
- **SPRT = CUSUM.** For a Gaussian mean shift delta (in sigma units) Page's CUSUM with allowance kappa = delta/2 is Wald's SPRT restarted at 0 (Page 1954, Lorden 1971). One implementation, kappa grid.
- **gps.cusum_ekf**: bank of 8 directional one-sided CUSUMs (the drift direction is unknown) on the standardised position innovation of the S3 ungated EKF; score = max over the bank, not reset after an alarm.
- **gps.cusum_dr** (the DR branch): state [position, Gauss-Markov receiver error, odometry scale s]; position moves by s x (odometry x heading displacement); no Doppler, no IMU. GPS position updates it only while trusted: it is FROZEN (update skipped, so neither the position estimate nor the scale can be dragged) while its own freeze-CUSUM >= 8 or the ungated EKF NIS is above chi2(0.999) (another layer). Scale prior N(1, 2%), learned only through GPS updates (L2c). The detection statistic is the NIS-CUSUM (chi-square excess) of its innovation.
- Variants for the ablation: `gps.cusum_dr_noscale` (2% scale error treated as noise, s fixed at 1), `gps.cusum_dr_nofreeze`.
- Frozen beforehand (not tuned in S4): receiver Gauss-Markov and EKF q_acc from S3 (TRAIN), heading 2 deg, odometry 0.05 m/s, scale prior 2%, scale random walk 2e-4 per fix, freeze-CUSUM allowance 0.5, freeze level 8. Tuned on the 16 drives (grid, LODO pooled threshold): kappa in {0.25, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2}, freeze in {never, self, self + NIS}, scale in {learn, none}; criterion J = mean of 7 run-level detections (6 attacks + slow drift within 30 s). Disclosure: the selection is not nested per fold, and the kappa grid was extended once (1.5, 2.0) because the first pass put the best at the edge; the gain is a plateau (J 0.690 at 1.0, 0.699 at 2.0).

### Reading the tables, plainly
- **LODO and the FA transfer.** On the development drives a threshold from the 3 val drives gives 3.10% FA on the 13 train drives for the EKF NIS (the S3 failure); LODO gives 1.11%. On test: EKF NIS 2.59% -> 0.44% (fixed, but its detection falls: drift 54% -> 26%, Doppler 97% -> 80%, because the pooled threshold is dominated by a few dynamic drives; one dev drive has 19.5% held-out FA, i.e. NIS has a drive-dependent scale). B2 goes the other way: 0.38% -> 1.58% (the pooled threshold is lower than the 3-drive one). The new CUSUM layers have flatter drive-to-drive FA on dev (worst held-out drive 1.8-2.0%) but on test clean-run FA is 2.53% (cusum_ekf) and 1.64% (cusum_dr) against an LODO estimate of about 1.0%: the test drives are harder than the average development drive. LODO fixes the EKF NIS transfer, does not fix it for cusum_ekf, and leaves cusum_dr just above the 1.5% goal.
- **Slow drift.** Target (>= 60% within 30 s at FA <= 1.5%): **NOT MET**. cusum_ekf detects 6 of 14 slow-drift test runs (43%) but at 2.53% FA; cusum_dr 2 of 14 (14%, same as B2 and the EKF); B2 2 of 14. Caveat that matters: **none of the 14 test slow-drift runs has a full 30 s window after onset** (KITTI test drives are 10-40 s long and the onset is uniform), so "within 30 s" is "within the rest of the run"; the target cannot be fully tested on this data. On development drives the numbers were 56% (cusum_ekf) and 44% (cusum_dr) of 34 runs, 6 with a full window; the test numbers are lower.
- **Where each layer wins (LODO calibration, run level, vs B2):** cusum_dr beats B2 on drift (+31 points), accelerating drift (+19), Doppler-consistent spoof (+29), meaconing (+13) at 1.64% FA vs 1.58% (within the 0.5 point rule); jump 100% for everything; freeze -4 points (no difference). cusum_ekf has the best drift detection (80%, 67% accelerating) but the worst Doppler spoof (23%) and meaconing (29%) because the EKF absorbs them; and 2.53% FA. The EKF NIS (LODO) beats B2 only on Doppler spoof (+39) and meaconing (+17).
- **Absorption (the main design result).** At onset+30 s the ungated EKF carries 99% (Doppler spoof) and 97% (meaconing) of the reported error; the DR branch without freezing carries 86% / 113% (it follows the attacker the same way); the frozen DR branch carries 9% / 21%, drift 6%, accelerating drift 2%, freeze 2%, jump 12% (B8 MET). The price of holding: clean-run FA of the DR layer 1.64% vs 1.83% without freezing, so freezing did not raise the FA; detection with freezing is 0 to +3 points run level and up to +7 points row level (Doppler spoof 60% vs 53%, meaconing 65% vs 58%; drift 44% vs 43%): small.
- **L2c.** Clean test runs: median scale error 0.70% at 10 s, 0.28% at 20 s, 0.24% at 30 s (n = 50 / 30 / 20 runs) from a prior error of 1.33% median (2.34% RMS): converges (B2 MET at 30 s; 60 s not evaluable, no test run is that long). Under attack, learning without freezing is dragged (error at onset+30 s: drift 7.6%, accelerating drift 7.7%, meaconing 7.1%, freeze attack 87.6%, Doppler spoof 2.4%); with freezing the error stays at its onset value or lower (1.6%, 0.5%, 0.5%, 1.2%, 0.4%). B3: the factor condition (<= 0.5x) holds for all four tested attacks, the absolute 1% condition fails for drift (1.60%, a single run whose scale error was already 2.07% at onset because the onset came 3 s after the start), so B3 is **met except the absolute bound on one run**. Scale learning matters for detection: without it (`cusum_dr_noscale`) drift detection falls to 20% and clean-run FA rises to 4.42% (LODO).
- **Harness (B2 + L1 + L2a base, logistic combiner fitted on the calibration set).** LODO: base AUC 0.900, detection 62.8%, FA 1.29%; + both L2b (S4_all) AUC 0.922, detection 70.6%, FA 1.14%; row-level gains drift +7.8, accelerating drift +4.3, jump +28.1 (CUSUM stays high after the jump), meaconing +10.3, Doppler spoof -3.0 points. B7 **MET under LODO** (AUC +0.022, FA 1.14%). Val-only: AUC 0.896 -> 0.903 (+0.007), FA 1.90% -> 2.20%: B7 **not met** as written (FA). The DR layer alone as the addition (S4_base_dr) gives AUC 0.917 / FA 1.12% (LODO): most of the gain comes from the DR branch, cusum_ekf adds mainly drift detection (S4_base_ekfcusum 54.6% drift row level) at higher FA.
- **UAV.** All EKF-CUSUM variants alarm at the first spoofed fix (118.000 s, delay 0 s) with 0/118 alarms before 117 s (B9 MET); benign flight 1-2 alarms of 248 (in-sample threshold), jamming flight 14-48 of 224 depending on variant.
- Delay distributions: see the tables; median delay on KITTI detected runs is 2 fixes for drift and 4 for accelerating drift (cusum_dr, cusum_ekf), versus 5 and 6 for B2.

## Problems, surprises, open questions
- Slow drift stays the open problem. The DR branch is limited by what the vehicle's own sensors know (heading noise 2 deg per fix is a random walk of about 0.35 m per second at 10 m/s, scale learned only after some driving). With drives this short a 30 s test window does not exist for most onsets: a dataset with long drives (or a separately generated long-run set of the same generator) is needed to test the target honestly. Question for the supervisor: accept the target as "not testable on KITTI" or request long runs (e.g. concatenated drives) in the next step.
- A false freeze latches (innovations are strongly autocorrelated during a hold, so the CUSUM runs away); the grid therefore chose a high freeze level (8) and the freeze mostly works through the EKF-NIS branch of the rule. Release logic is only "the statistic decays".
- LODO does not make FA transfer perfect: the 16-drive estimate of the FA for new drives is 1.0-1.3% and the test gives 0.4-2.5% depending on the layer. Per-drive FA varies by more than 10x for NIS-type scores. Proposal: report LODO worst-drive FA next to the pooled number, and in S8 re-run B2 / L1 / L2a with LODO (B2 gets worse with it: 0.38% -> 1.58%).
- The selection of the DR configuration was done on the pooled 16 drives, not nested per fold (disclosed above).
- **Proposal for common/**: `gps/lodo.py::lodo_report(scores, groups, budget)` is generic (pooled tie-safe threshold, per-held-out-group threshold from the other groups, held-out FA); candidate for `common/calibration.py`. Not edited there.
- Behaviour check: the dry run of tools/WP1_S4_test.py on validation drives (substituted for test) was used only to find code bugs (before the real pass); the test pass was run once.
