### Rules and physical limits (fixed before any run; sources in the module docstring)
Max speed 70 m/s (250 km/h manufacturer limit); longitudinal acceleration 1.3 g and lateral 1.2 g (tyre friction, Wong; Rajamani; 0-60 mph in 2.1 s for the quickest cars); jerk 50 m/s^3 (ENGINEERING ESTIMATE, not a literature value: full braking cannot build up in under ~0.25 s); yaw rate <= v / 5 m (minimum turn radius, gated at v >= 3 m/s); >= 4 satellites (four unknowns); HDOP <= 5 (Langley 1999); fix type >= 3; receiver clock change <= 1 ms/s. Accelerations are computed from BOTH the reported position steps and the reported Doppler velocity (max of the two), at 1 Hz: they are averages over a second, so the limits are bounds, not typical values.
Data-driven (validation only): Doppler-vs-position consistency (position step minus mean of the two Doppler velocities, m/s); one-step jumps of satellites, HDOP, noise_per_ms, jamming_indicator.

- A4 by construction. Note the data-driven Doppler-vs-position rule has 1.26% FA on clean test runs (1.11% on all normal test rows) against its 1% validation budget.
- A5 MET: AV-GPS kinematic union 0.61% FA (33 of 5415 normal rows; real receiver glitches, 27 of them long_accel).
- A6 PARTLY MET. Detection part MET: sat_jump, hdop_jump, noise_jump and vel_pos_consistency fire at the first spoofed fix (118.0 s, delay 0.0003 s). The jump thresholds come from only 248 benign fixes; not retuned (rule 5.4).
- A7 MET: B2 + all L1: AUC 0.860 vs 0.847 for B2 alone, test FA 1.39% (budget 1%).

## What L1 adds on top of B2 (harness ablation on KITTI)
- Physical kinematic rules: no false alarms, but they detect little: jump (86% of runs, the large jumps), freeze 14%, drift / accel_drift / Doppler spoof / meaconing 0%. Useful as a free safety net (and for receiver glitches), but in the combiner they add nothing measurable to B2 (B2 + kinematic: AUC 0.855 vs 0.847, FA 0.99%; every all_but_x difference is about 1 point or less).
- The gain comes from the receiver self-consistency rule (Doppler vs position). B2 + it: row detection drift 29.8 -> 44.0%, accel_drift 43.7 -> 53.9%, FA 0.70 -> 1.42% (noisy-or). Full 7-layer logistic combination: drift 39.7, accel_drift 49.9, meaconing 28.7 -> 33.7, Doppler spoof 44.3 -> 46.2, FA 1.39%. Removing B2 from the full set drops AUC 0.860 -> 0.709: L1 complements B2, it does not replace it.
- As designed, the Doppler-consistent spoof and the meaconing delay are NOT caught by L1 (Doppler-vs-position: 16% / 17% of runs, around its false-alarm level): position and velocity stay consistent with each other, only vehicle sensors (odometry, IMU) can expose them. That is S3-S5.
- The Doppler-vs-position result depends on our simulated receiver (Doppler noise 0.05 m/s, position AR(1) noise) and on a naive attacker; an attacker that fakes velocity as well defeats it (that is the Doppler spoof). On the real UAV receiver it is the strongest single rule (AUC 0.98 for fixes after 118 s).
- Detection delay with Doppler-vs-position (run-level medians): jump 0 s, freeze 1 s, drift 2 s, accel_drift 4 s (B2: 5 s and 7 s for drift / accel_drift).

## Per-dataset summary
- KITTI: receiver-field rules not evaluable. Thinnest margin to a physical limit: yaw rate (0.87 of its limit on validation normal rows, 0.77 on test clean runs); the other four stay below 0.33.
- AV-GPS: the kinematic physical rules flag real receiver glitches in normal data at 0.61% and detect 13.7% of the spoofed rows (spoofed sessions contain position steps from metres to millions of metres, i.e. placeholder coordinates; only 1.2% of attack rows have a step over 100 km). The satellite / HDOP rules reach about 99% only because spoofed sessions are the no-fix placeholder (hdop = 100, sats = 0): ARTEFACT, not evidence.
- UAV (real HackRF spoofing): after the first spoofed fix 68% of fixes are flagged by the union (2.5% before). The jamming flight has no onset label: rules fire in a burst at 172-209 s (fix_type 0 at 175 s, HDOP rise); two earlier isolated alarms (26 s, 39 s) cannot be interpreted without labels.

## Problems, surprises, open questions
1. Decision for the supervisor: all UAV flights are test in splits.json. The UAV jump / consistency rules need normal data of that receiver, so I used the BENIGN flight as their calibration set (in-sample union FA 4 of 248) and measured out-of-sample FA on the pre-onset part of the spoofing flight (A6 FA part not met, 2.54%). splits.json unchanged. Please confirm or give another rule.
2. A6 FA part not met; thresholds not retuned. The noise / jamming-indicator jump thresholds are probably too tight for a 248-fix calibration set.
3. The jerk limit (50 m/s^3) is an engineering estimate, not a literature value; on KITTI it detects nothing that long_accel does not.
4. Per-rule 1% budget operating points transfer imperfectly (yaw_rate 4.16% and lat_accel 3.70% test FA at their 1% validation threshold in the harness, validation drives have few large values): this is why the physical-limit operating point (FA 0.00%) is reported separately.
5. AV-GPS time is a 1 Hz row counter (rate checked: median position step / odometry speed about 1).
