Recording data quality (results/WP8/S8/dq.json, 13 H31 + 10 V31_val + 13 v3 train drives; OXTS fields of the KITTI dataformat):
- OXTS reference 0093: `pos_accuracy` 18.7-44.3 m on EVERY frame (all 35 other drives: max 1.62 m, 12 H31 others <= 0.89 m); `vel_accuracy` max 1.17 m/s (others <= 0.16); numsats 2-7 (mean 4.5); posmode 0 (no GNSS position) on 258 of 433 frames; orimode 0 on all 433 frames; velmode 0 on 313.
- Physics of the OXTS record itself: leftward velocity `vl` reaches 14.1 m/s (a car cannot slide sideways at that speed; every other drive <= 0.26 m/s); the yaw angle differs from the course over ground atan2(vn, ve) by 9 deg at the start, 67 deg at t = 15 s, 124 deg mean (up to 160 deg) at t = 27 s: the INS heading never converged (no GNSS orientation at the start with 2 satellites). The forward speed vf (the projection of the velocity on that wrong heading) is 3.2-5 m/s while |v| = 8-14 m/s (it even goes to -6.2 m/s at t = 27 s); the position track jumps (1 s position-difference speed 22 m/s at t = 15 s, when the receiver re-acquires).
- Independent witness: the LiDAR ICP odometry (never uses OXTS) has lateral step 0.009 m per scan and yaw rate equal to the OXTS gyro (0.024 vs 0.024 rad/s, ...), speed 5.3-6.3 m/s where vf says 3.2-4.8. Step error OXTS pose vs ICP per scan: 0093 mean 0.99 m (p95 1.99 m); all 22 other drives (H31 + V31_val) 0.007-0.09 m. Timestamps / counts are clean on all drives (0093: 433 OXTS = 433 scans, OXTS dt max 0.12 s, scan dt max 0.104 s, no duplicates, scans 117.7 k points mean vs 107-126 k elsewhere).
- Driving envelope of 0093 is NOT out of range: mean speed 4.0 m/s (max 8.65), |yaw rate| 95th pct 0.355 rad/s (max 0.52) inside the training range (0.66), a city drive like many training drives. Calibration: same day 2011_09_26 as the v3 train drives (calib files of the day, unchanged).
- LiDAR odometry itself is healthy on 0093 (inlier fraction 0.71, rms 0.017-0.024 m, comparable to other drives).

Mechanism. In this evaluation every vehicle-block channel except the LiDAR is DERIVED from the OXTS record (gps.stream.make_run: GPS position = OXTS position + simulated noise, Doppler = OXTS velocity, compass = OXTS yaw, odometry = OXTS vf, gyro = OXTS wz; the simulated CAN wheel speed = OXTS vf; the ego pose of LiDAR layers L2c background / L2d tracking = OXTS lat / lon / yaw, lidar/ego.py). When the OXTS solution itself is wrong, all these "independent" sensors are wrong in the same way and disagree with the real LiDAR scene: GPS layers (position vs heading vs odometry vs LiDAR) fire, the speed edges against LiDAR fire, and the LiDAR tracker / background layers that compensate the previous scan with the OXTS pose see surfaces "appear in free space" and "follow the sensor" (L2d fires in the OXTS-vs-ICP error scans: mean error 1.11 m when firing vs 0.25 m when not).
Classification: (a) DATA DEFECT in the recording (the reference navigation solution of this drive never converged and reports it itself, `pos_accuracy` 19-44 m); it is not (c) a detector bug (each detector reports a genuine inconsistency between its inputs; the same layers stay at ~1 % on the 35 drives with a healthy reference, including 0095 / 0106 which have posmode 0 but pos_accuracy <= 1.6 m and a consistent heading), and not (b) an out-of-envelope driving regime. Caveat that must stay in the report: in a real vehicle the CAN wheel speed does not come from the navigation unit; the sim couples them, so the number of alarming cross edges on 0093 is larger than a real vehicle with a degraded GNSS/INS unit would produce (the GNSS and pose based checks would fire, the CAN-vs-LiDAR edge would not). The finding that matters is that the GNSS/INS-based inputs are invalid and say so.

## 3. Allowed fix and pre-registered rules (frozen here; nothing below may be changed after the H31 re-scoring)

Fix F1 "reference-health gate" (a sensor-health signal, NOT an attack verdict). Label-free, physical, no refit, no change of any score, ECDF, threshold, combiner or correlator.
- Degraded OXTS frame: `pos_accuracy` > 5.0 m OR `vel_accuracy` > 0.5 m/s (fields 23 and 24 of the OXTS record, the unit's own accuracy estimate). Thresholds: 3 x the largest value seen on any v3-train / V31_val drive (1.62 m, 0.16 m/s; dq.json), rounded; physical anchor: the GPS detector's receiver-noise model is sigma = 1 m per axis, 0.05 m/s Doppler (gps/stream.py), so a unit that reports 5 m / 0.5 m/s (5 sigma / 10 sigma of that model) cannot be tested with those residual gates. Fixed now; the sensitivity (range of P in which the H31 outcome is identical) is reported afterwards as a description, not as a choice: on all 36 other drives the gate fires on 0 frames for any P in (1.62, 18.67) m.
- Tick gate: a 1 Hz tick (window (t_k - 1, t_k]) is "reference-degraded" if at least one OXTS frame in the window is degraded. Scan gate: a LiDAR scan is degraded if its (time-nearest) OXTS frame is.
- Effect at degraded ticks / scans: GPS domain score 0 and own alarm False; cross domain score 0, edge scores 0, own alarm False, blame None, observed False; LiDAR layers L2c and L2d (they use the OXTS ego pose) are masked out of the union (stat = max over L1 and L2a only), L1 / L2a / pose-free layers stay active; CAN, network, V2X untouched. Each degraded tick publishes `sensor_health = reference degraded` (reported separately: health-flag ticks and episodes per hour and the blind share of ticks; they are NOT alert events and NOT counted in FA).
- Not changed: any LiDAR L2a / V2AIX / comma / start-up transient behaviour (section 2). Dropping drives or choosing thresholds on H31 numbers are not allowed and are not done.

Acceptance rules for S8 (V31_val decides; the H31 row is descriptive):
- A1 (no harm on V31_val, K family of the S2 construction): the gate fires on <= 0.5 % of the V31_val ticks and scans (expected 0); K mean event detection (single-domain types, S2 definition) drops by <= 1.0 pt and K clean FA / events per hour do not increase vs the frozen v3.1 (expected identical).
- A2 (gate precision on healthy material): the gate fires on <= 0.5 % of the frames of the 13 v3-train + 10 V31_val drives (expected 0).
- A3 (regression): the frozen-v3 and frozen-v3.1 numbers of S7 are reproduced exactly with the gate disabled (new modules only; v3 / S7 files untouched).
- A4 (H31, exposure 2): one re-scoring, labelled "re-used hold-out, exposure 2 (post-M4 fix), NOT fresh", frozen v3.1 vs v3.1 + S8 side by side on the same composites, paired group bootstrap over drives (B = 2000, seed 31). An improvement is claimed only for rows whose paired CI excludes 0 in the good direction. The M4 verdict of S7 is NOT overwritten; M4 bounds are quoted for information.
- A5 (attack windows): report the share of attack-composite ticks and scans that are degraded (per domain / family) and the detection on attack ticks that overlap degraded ticks; the gate counts as a miss there (no credit). Discuss the exploit surface (accuracy fields are receiver output).
- Pre-registered expectation (written before the result): K clean tick FA falls from 12.2 % to about 4.5 %, events per clean hour to about 24 /h, i.e. M4-1 / M4-2 for K stay NOT MET; LiDAR scan FA falls from 8.4 % to about 3 % (bound 1.5 %, NOT MET); comma, V2AIX, CAN rows are unchanged. This fix repairs the 0093 defect, it does not make v3.1 meet M4.
