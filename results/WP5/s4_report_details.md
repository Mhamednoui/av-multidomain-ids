# WP5 S4: L3 per-sender Kalman tracker with innovation tests

Date: 2026-10-20    Worker: sonnet (medium)

## Goal of the step
Per-sender Kalman tracker whose noise is fit on genuine TRAIN senders only, with per-message NIS (chi-square) and a per-sender CUSUM on the NIS, aimed at the classes S1-S3 missed (ConstPosOffset 2, EventualStop 9, DelayedMessages 12 (held out), DataReplay 11, GridSybil 16, DataReplaySybil 17 (held out)); filter consistency on genuine validation; per-message-pseudonym Sybil (18 / 19) cannot be seen by a tracker (optional track-birth check). VeReMi is the primary benchmark; Tampa is an illustration (3 test vehicles, 2 attackers per type), no statistical claims.

## Acceptance rule (notes/WP5_v2x.md, 'S4 L3 per-sender Kalman tracker: acceptance rule', written 2026-10-20 before any S4 detection result)
S4 MET if: (1) filter, equations, layers, tests, noise fit on genuine TRAIN only, held-out 6 / 12 / 17 in no calibration; (2) NIS consistency on genuine val reported (plainly stated if NIS mean outside [3, 5] or fraction above chi2 95% outside [2%, 10%]); (3) false alarms on genuine TEST messages <= 1.5% (VeReMi) / <= 2% (Tampa) for every L3 check and the L3 union; (4) per-class detection (message and sender level), delay, FA, ablation on top of L1+L2; (5) value: L1+L2+L3 beats L1+L2 by >= 3 points of mean detection over the 19 classes at <= 1.5% FA; (6) tests pass. Expectations K1-K7 were pre-registered (evaluated below).

### Model (equations)
State s = [x, y, vx, vy], measurement z = [x, y, vx, vy] (velocity = speed x unit heading), H = I.
- predict: s- = F s, F = [[I, dt I], [0, I]]; P- = F P F^T + Q, Q = sa^2 [[dt^3/3 I, dt^2/2 I], [dt^2/2 I, dt I]] (white-noise acceleration = constant-velocity model).
- innovation nu = z - s-, S = P- + R, R = diag(sp^2, sp^2, sv^2, sv^2), NIS = nu^T S^-1 nu, chi2(4) if the model is right.
- update K = P- S^-1, s = s- + K nu, P = (I - K) P-. Init at a segment's first message (s = z, P = R, not scored). A segment restarts after a gap > 5 s (the L2 rule).
- CUSUM per segment: hi C_k = max(0, C_(k-1) + NIS_k - 9.488) (chi2(4) 95% quantile); lo C_k = max(0, C_(k-1) + 1.923 - NIS_k) (chi2(4) 25% quantile: "too exact" tracks). Constants fixed in the rule, not tuned.
- Noise (sp, sv, sa): maximum likelihood of the innovations on 800 random genuine TRAIN tracks (first 120 messages each; Tampa: 37 train tracks), floors sp >= 0.05 m, sv >= 0.02 m/s. CTRA not implemented (none of the targeted attacks is about turn dynamics; the velocity measurement corrects the heading each beacon).
- track_birth: for the first message of a track, the number of other new identities within +-300 m (claimed positions, Chebyshev) in the last 10 s; no receiver position needed.
- Thresholds: genuine validation only, 1% budget, tie-safe; held-out types 6, 12, 17 removed from the validation frame. L3 union = max of calibrated L3 scores at ONE 1% budget.

- 11 DataReplay: 97.6% (L1+L2 68.4%). The replayed values are kinematically inconsistent with the attacker's own track in VeReMi (as S3 found); kf_nis alone 67.5% (= pos_speed), the CUSUM adds the persistence after a jump.
- 12 DelayedMessages (held out): 48% by cusum_hi (L1+L2 1.4%), kf_nis only 1.6%. The zero stream is consistent and NOT detected; the alarm comes at the jump from the origin to the real position and then persists (CUSUM memory: 90% of the alarmed messages have their own NIS below the 95% bound). Sender level (>= 3 alarms) 77% vs 15%, median delay of detected attackers 61 s (the length of the placeholder phase). So L3 sees the placeholder-to-real transition, not "delay".
- 16 GridSybil: 73.3% (L1+L2 61.5%). Ghosts' reported speed does not match their displacement (VeReMi implementation); the tracker re-detects what L2 saw and a little more.
- 17 DataReplaySybil (held out): tracker 14% / 12%; track_birth 49%; L3 union 52.7% vs 17.4%. Median track length 1 message (55% of tracks have exactly one message).
- 18 / 19 DoS(Random / Disruptive)Sybil: the tracker is blind (0.0% / 0.3%): one to two messages per pseudonym, 50 s apart (> the 5 s gap rule). At sender level (>= 3 alarms) L3 union catches 97.8% / 100% of these attackers (L1+L2: 0% / 37%), with 2.9% of genuine senders flagged. Median delay 2.5 s / 1.0 s.
- Not targeted but changed: ConstPos 86 -> 96%, ConstSpeed 96 -> 99%, ConstSpeedOffset* 86 -> 99% (L3 union vs L2 union; DoS 74 -> 98%, where the L1 rate check already gave 99.7%).

### Honest limitations of these numbers
1. CUSUM memory: after one big jump the CUSUM stays above its threshold for many messages (it decays by only 9.5 per message after a NIS of thousands), so message-level detection of the CUSUM counts messages after the event too. This inflates classes with an isolated jump (12) and helps classes with persistent inconsistency (11, 13, 16). The kf_nis rows are the "own-message" view. Not changed (no re-tuning).
2. VeReMi's own implementations of 11, 13, 16 are kinematically inconsistent, so their high detection says little about a careful replayer. On Tampa (injected, consistent) the same tracker detects DataReplay 99% and ConstPos / ConstSpeed ~99% where L2 had 8 / 10 / 3.5%, because the tracker also tests velocity against position at 10 Hz with a tight velocity tolerance; the mechanism on Tampa DataReplay was not examined (illustration only: 3 vehicles).
3. cusum_lo is not deployable on real data: Tampa genuine test vehicles that stand still repeat positions exactly, so it alarms on 13.5% of genuine test messages although its validation FA is 1% (the validation part has too little standstill). On VeReMi its FA is fine (0.7%) because the simulator adds fresh noise every beacon. The Tampa L3 union FA failure is entirely this check. Recommendation: drop cusum_lo from the deployable L3 unless calibrated on real standstill data; the harness all-but-one row without it has VeReMi 81.1% at 1.07% FA and Tampa 69.2% at 2.82% FA.
4. track_birth: FA per genuine birth is 61.6% on val and 80.8% on test (the 1% budget is per message and genuine births are 0.9% of messages, so the check is lenient: it flags a new identity whenever another new identity appeared nearby in the last 10 s). It is only meaningful as a sender-level count (>= 3 alarms) against identity churn. VeReMi genuine senders keep one pseudonym for the whole trip; real V2X rotates pseudonyms every few minutes, so real genuine births are far more frequent and this check would need real calibration. On Tampa 18 / 19 are detected 58% / 53% by the TRACKER only because the injection draws 100 pseudonyms per message at ~20 Hz, so each pseudonym reappears within 5 s; in VeReMi it does not. S20_StaleReplay 97% by track_birth is a construction artefact (each stale message is its own track), not credited.
5. A per-sender view with >= 1 alarm is useless at a per-message 1% budget (50% of genuine VeReMi senders have >= 1 alarm); use the >= 3 alarm table.
6. NIS shape: heavy-tailed on moving vehicles (acceleration changes are not white noise) and degenerate-small on standing ones; the MLE drives sp and sv to their floors (VeReMi position noise is strongly time-correlated: median |displacement - speed x dt| = 0.14 m for sigma 4 m). The tracker therefore tests position-velocity consistency rather than measurement noise. A model using the reported acceleration (CA / CTRA) would give a more chi2-like NIS; not done (no targeted class depends on it).
7. Disclosure: the idea of the "too exact" test (cusum_lo) came from looking at TRAIN rows (including types 12 and 17) before the rule was written; nothing of the held-out types entered any fit or threshold. Tampa conclusions: 3 test vehicles, 2 attackers per type; the bootstrap CIs are not valid intervals.

## Problems, surprises, open questions
1. Noise fit takes ~9 minutes (batched numpy, 304 function evaluations); fine for a one-off, not for repeated use.
2. Question: a learned road-occupancy prior (cells visited by genuine TRAIN traffic) would likely catch the cases a tracker cannot (2 ConstPosOffset, 12 zero placeholders, 18 / 19 random positions) with no receiver position; it is the VeReMi counterpart of map_road and shares its false-alarm risk. Not built (outside S4). Do you want it?
3. Question: keep cusum_lo in the deployable L3 (VeReMi only) or drop it? My recommendation: drop it and report it as a VeReMi-only indicator.
4. DATASETS.md / other shared files not touched.
